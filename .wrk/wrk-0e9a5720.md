Implemented the requested compact right-sidebar layer controls.

- Removed visible Active/Hidden badges, order caption, standalone rename/reorder/visibility controls, and explanatory layer footer copy.
- Added a borderless + in the header, row drag grips, right-aligned eye/eye-off and trash icons, and borderless inline name fields. Clicking/tabbing into a name selects that layer; editing saves on Enter or blur, Escape cancels, and blank names revert. The highlighted active row remains.
- Dragging only starts from grips, shows an insertion line, supports scrolling at the sidebar edges, and commits an arbitrary stack move as one history operation. Escape, lost/canceled pointer capture, outside drops, and unchanged positions preserve history. Grips also accept Up/Down/Home/End. Reorder, hide, and removal of another row preserve the current editing target.
- Preserved populated-layer confirmation, last-layer protection, undo/redo, layer identities/tiles/registry associations, native text shortcuts, and restored focus after removal. Updated related browser workflows and layer documentation.
