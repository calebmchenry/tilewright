## Implementation and decisions

- `src/domain/agent-export.ts`: independent public codec, importing neither the
  project codec/schema nor editor/renderer modules. Synchronous committed-revision
  capture freezes detached sparse runs, resolves current registry colors and
  meanings, retains unused registry entries and distinguishes raw paint with
  explicit null meaning. All layers, empty margins, hidden/underlying content and
  complete independent area metadata survive.
- Canonical layer/run ordering comes from the sparse model; color sorting/remapping
  and ASCII object-key serialization produce deterministic UTF-8 plus one LF.
  The encoder yields between bounded string/byte batches, supports cancellation,
  enforces all independent v1 capacity ceilings and rejects forged snapshots.
  Invalid metadata, unknown source fields, ambiguous registry ownership, invalid
  areas and dangling registry references fail without partial output or mutation.
- `src/editor/session.ts`: `captureAgentExportSnapshot` checks the session is open
  and captures only its history; no viewport, active/hidden preferences, selection,
  clipboard, preview, filename or overview cache participates. Export never marks
  a project saved/recovered or changes undo/redo state.
- `tests/fixtures/agent-export.ts`, `tests/domain/agent-export.test.ts` and
  `tests/domain/agent-export-snapshot.test.ts`: 17 focused tests, including the
  reviewed golden hash, recolor/undo/redo, all tiers, empty layers, negative/grown
  bounds, sparse gaps, cross-partition coalescing, integer/Unicode extremes,
  capacities, cancellation, invalid sources and concurrent editing through async
  encoding. Actual session/view/selection/draft/cache states yield identical bytes.
- `docs/agent-export-format.md`, `src/domain/agent-export-format.ts`, `README.md`:
  API, decisions, measurements and integration boundary. Corrected two stale
  TW-043 prose assignments of native publication to TW-044: this ticket's explicit
  next step assigns native action/progress and atomic writes to TW-045. This ticket
  implements the codec and cancellation signal, without a new native action.
