## Outcome

Serialize the complete map into the selected agent-readable schema.

## Source and scope

**Source:** INTENT.md — Project files and agent exports; INTENT.md — First-version success criteria.

**Execution order:** 44 of 54. Phase: export.

**Prerequisite:** [wrk-eeabc6ef](wrk-eeabc6ef.md) — TW-043: Specify a separate whole-map agent-export schema.

This is an intentionally sequential delivery backlog. The dependency includes earlier prerequisites transitively; complete it before starting this ticket.

Implementation details left open by INTENT.md are decisions to document, not additional confirmed user requirements.

## Work

- Implement an export codec independent of the editable-project encoder and renderer.
- Traverse sparse committed content in deterministic order, preserving every layer and registry/area meaning.
- Support coherent revision snapshots and efficient encoding for large maps.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Whole-map dimensions, coordinates, named layer order, occupied cells, and underlying/hidden content are preserved.
- [ ] Registry names/current colors and unlabeled-color handling match the schema with no ambiguous or dangling references.
- [ ] Named-area geometry, names, descriptions, and required label/tier semantics survive export.
- [ ] Viewport, active layer, selection, overview caches, and unsaved filename do not limit exported content or become interpretation requirements.

## Validation

- Validate golden exports for raw/registered colors, recoloring, sparse gaps, overlapping layers, and named areas.
- Export with different viewport/selection/visibility states and assert complete semantic coverage.

Record the implementation/decision location and actual validation evidence in this ticket before marking it done.
