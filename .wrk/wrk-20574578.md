## Outcome

Let authors choose map dimensions and deliberately add space without automatic expansion.

## Source and scope

**Source:** INTENT.md — Map size and navigation; INTENT.md — First-version success criteria; INTENT.md — Remaining implementation decisions.

**Execution order:** 12 of 54. Phase: canvas.

**Prerequisite:** [wrk-02942808](wrk-02942808.md) — TW-011: Pass the early populated-map rendering and architecture performance gate.

This is an intentionally sequential delivery backlog. The dependency includes earlier prerequisites transitively; complete it before starting this ticket.

Implementation details left open by INTENT.md are decisions to document, not additional confirmed user requirements.

## Work

- Implement New Map dimensions and documented defaults with validation and tile/chunk size feedback.
- Implement explicit growth controls using the established direction/increment policy and coordinate convention.
- Route growth through history and update camera limits, grids, and overview caches.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Valid maps can be created at small, non-chunk-aligned, and approximately 6,400 by 6,400 dimensions.
- [ ] Invalid/unsafe dimensions are explained without allocating an accidental huge dense canvas.
- [ ] Growth preserves existing layers, registry associations, and annotation positions according to the coordinate contract; new space is empty.
- [ ] Painting/dragging outside bounds never grows the map; growth undo/redo restores bounds and content consistently.

## Validation

- Test dimensions, every supported growth direction, coordinate stability, boundary edits, and undo/redo.
- Grow a populated multilayer fixture and confirm its original content still renders at the expected positions.

Record the implementation/decision location and actual validation evidence in this ticket before marking it done.
