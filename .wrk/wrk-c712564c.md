## Outcome

Remove tile content and reuse colors without losing registered meaning.

## Source and scope

**Source:** INTENT.md — Drawing and rearranging; INTENT.md — Map use and meaning.

**Execution order:** 20 of 54. Phase: painting.

**Prerequisite:** [wrk-8e1860da](wrk-8e1860da.md) — TW-019: Implement the adjustable-size pencil.

This is an intentionally sequential delivery backlog. The dependency includes earlier prerequisites transitively; complete it before starting this ticket.

Implementation details left open by INTENT.md are decisions to document, not additional confirmed user requirements.

## Work

- Implement erasing through the drawing gesture pipeline with clear brush behavior.
- Choose and document eyedropper sampling rules for active versus visible composite layers and empty cells.
- Sample a tile into either its existing registry association or a raw paint color, preserving semantic identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Erasing clears active-layer cells to empty and reveals underlying content; it does not paint a background-colored tile.
- [ ] Erase strokes are atomic and undo restores exactly the removed content.
- [ ] Sampling registered grass selects the grass entry so later paint follows future recolors.
- [ ] Sampling raw/empty/hidden-layer cases follows the documented rule without mutating the document.

## Validation

- Test erase across boundaries and overlapping layers, followed by undo/redo.
- Sample registered and unregistered colors, paint with them, then recolor the entry to verify identity behavior.

Record the implementation/decision location and actual validation evidence in this ticket before marking it done.
