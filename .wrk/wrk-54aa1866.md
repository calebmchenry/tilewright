## Outcome

Reuse rectangular content while preserving layers, colors, and registered meaning.

## Source and scope

**Source:** INTENT.md — Drawing and rearranging; INTENT.md — First-version success criteria.

**Execution order:** 26 of 54. Phase: selection.

**Prerequisite:** [wrk-9bab6495](wrk-9bab6495.md) — TW-025: Implement overlap-safe selection dragging and whole-tile nudging.

This is an intentionally sequential delivery backlog. The dependency includes earlier prerequisites transitively; complete it before starting this ticket.

Implementation details left open by INTENT.md are decisions to document, not additional confirmed user requirements.

## Work

- Implement clipboard payloads and cut/copy/paste actions using the documented clipboard and layer-mapping policy.
- Provide a paste preview/anchor and clear commit/cancel behavior, including repeated paste.
- Preserve source registry identities and handle cross-document or stale-layer references according to the contract.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Copy leaves document/history unchanged; cut removes only included-layer cells with a reversible command.
- [ ] Paste preserves relative geometry, separate layers, raw colors, and registry associations.
- [ ] Empty-cell and collision behavior follows the contract; overwritten destination data is restored by undo.
- [ ] Clipboard operations contain no annotations, obey fixed bounds, and do not override text-field cut/copy/paste.

## Validation

- Test cut then cancel paste, repeated paste, layer removal after copy, boundaries, and any supported cross-document case.
- Copy a layered house, paste it, recolor its registry entry, and verify both copies update correctly.

Record the implementation/decision location and actual validation evidence in this ticket before marking it done.
