## Outcome

Resolve interaction details while preserving the explicit movement rules in the intent.

This is an intentionally sequential delivery backlog. The dependency includes earlier prerequisites transitively; complete it before starting this ticket.

Implementation details left open by INTENT.md are decisions to document, not additional confirmed user requirements.

## Work

- Specify rectangle endpoints, drag/keyboard behavior, chosen-layer membership, and hidden-layer handling.
- Decide clipboard scope and layer mapping, paste anchoring, repeated paste, transform pivot, and non-square rotation dimensions.
- Choose explicit fixed-boundary behavior for movement/paste/transform and consistent empty-cell compositing rules for non-move operations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Movement uses a snapshot: occupied source cells overwrite same-layer destinations; source holes leave destination content untouched.
- [ ] Overlapping moves are defined without read-after-write loss, and undo restores cleared sources and overwritten destinations.
- [ ] Active layer is the default; selected multiple layers retain their identities and offsets while unrelated layers remain unchanged.
- [ ] Annotations are excluded from selection payloads and transforms. Decisions cover Escape/cancel, cut without paste, cross-document clipboard behavior, and rejected boundary operations.

## Validation

- Document worked examples for an 8 by 8 house moved three tiles, an overlapping move with holes, and a non-square quarter-turn.
- Check that no decision introduces freeform selection or automatic annotation movement.

Record the implementation/decision location and actual validation evidence in this ticket before marking it done.
